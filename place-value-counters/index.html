How to use this tool
  • Type a number into Row 1 or Row 2 and press Set to build it from counters (decimals allowed, e.g. 3452.6).
  • Drag a counter sideways into the next column to exchange/regroup it: drag it right (into the smaller-value neighbour) and it becomes 10 of that value; drag it left (into the bigger-value neighbour) and, if there are 10 or more of that counter in its column, they combine into 1.
  • Drag a counter up or down between Row 1 and Row 2 to reposition it without changing its value — useful for lining up a calculation across both rows.
  • Double-click or double-tap a counter to remove it — useful for modelling subtraction. (You can also drag it onto the bin, bottom right.)
  • Tap the dashed + strip at the top of any column to add another counter there — useful for modelling addition.
  • Tap a tenths/hundredths/thousandths counter to flip it, just like the real ones — it swaps between the decimal (e.g. 0.01) and fraction (e.g. 1⁄100) side.
  • The ×10 / ÷10 buttons shift every counter in a row one column at once — great for showing what happens to a number when you multiply or divide by 10.
  • A column glows orange once it holds 10 or more counters — a hint it's time to exchange.
  • Use Columns to show only the place-value range a class needs (e.g. just Hundreds/Tens/Ones for a younger group) — your choice is remembered next time you open this page on the same device.
  • Use Hide numbers & controls to tuck away the totals and Row 1/Row 2 panel once you've set your numbers up, so the board is just the counters — handy for a clean whiteboard display.
