How to use this: Pick a year group above. Each row shows a sentence structure from the National Curriculum, broken into colour-coded parts using Jacobs Education's Colour-Coded Sentence Progression. Tap any coloured block and type your own word or phrase — the sentence below rebuilds itself as you go. Use a class's own year group for whole-class teaching, or drop down/up a year group to scaffold or stretch individual pupils. Or try the ✏️ My sentence tab to have any sentence you write auto-coloured. On the whiteboard, use 🎬 Present on any sentence to reveal it block by block, full screen.
