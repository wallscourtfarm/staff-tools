Oak Clips

 Songs and videos from Oak lessons, ready for PowerPoint
Copy the address from the top of the Oak page in your browser. A unit link shows every lesson in the unit.

Putting a clip into PowerPoint

  1. Press ⬇ Save next to a clip, or ⬇ Save all for a whole lesson (a zip file: open it to get the clips out).
  2. In PowerPoint: Insert → Video (or Audio) → This Device, and choose the clip from your Downloads folder.
  3. The song plays on the same slide as its words, so there's no need to switch to the website.
Oak's lessons are free to use, and the songs in them are cleared for teaching in UK schools. Keep the files in school: don't post them online or share them outside school.
