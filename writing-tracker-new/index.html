How it works

1. For each statement, you pick one status (click a cell). The tool never works the status out for you.

StatusWhat it meansShare of the statement's weight earned
SecureTaught and used accurately across multiple pieces of writing (over ¾ accurate)100%
DevelopingUsing it with increasing success (about ⅔ accurate)60%
EmergingUsed a few times with some success (about ½ accurate)40%
ReteachNo or very little accuracy (0–25%); explicitly reteach0%
Taught, not usedTaught, not yet seen in their writing0% (counts)
x Not taught · UnreviewedNo evidence yetnot counted at all

2. Percentages. A child's Expected % = weight earned ÷ weight available across the non-negotiables and expected standards that count. Greater depth % is worked out separately from the greater depth statements. Non-negotiables are part of the percentage; there is no pass/fail on them.

3. Grade for a child working at their own year group: D = expected ≥ 90% and greater depth ≥ 60% · O1 = expected ≥ 75% · O2 = expected ≥ 65% · Y = expected ≥ 50%.

4. Below 50% of the year group's expected statements means "at an earlier stage": A - Yn. The teacher chooses which year's statements a child is working at (the "working at" box under the child's initials). If a child on an earlier year's statements is still under 50% of them, the tool flags it and offers to move them one year lower. It is always the teacher's decision. Below Y1 is recorded as A - EY.

5. Weights (0 to 10, whole numbers) are shown next to each statement. They were agreed by staff on 23.10.26 and are locked, so they cannot be changed here. "Agreed weights" under the grid lets you download them.

Marking with the keyboard: click a cell, then type R Reteach, E Emerging, D Developing, S Secure, X Not taught, T Taught, not used, or Delete for Unreviewed. The mark is set and the highlight moves down to the next statement for the same child. The arrow keys move the highlight; Esc lets go of it.

6. Terms. The weights in the boxes are the Term 1 and 2 weights, so the non-negotiables can count for more early in the year. From Term 3, every non-negotiable has a weight of 1; all other weights stay the same. The tool reads the current term from the school calendar and uses the right weights automatically. Each term's results always use that term's weights, so a Term 2 result keeps its Term 2 figure when you look back at it later. From Term 3 the non-negotiable weights show as 1 and cannot be changed here.
