Privacy Policy

WFA Cover & Release Planner — notification service

What this tool is

The Cover & Release Planner is an internal scheduling tool used by staff at Wallscourt Farm Academy to plan cover arrangements and release time. This page covers the notification service that emails staff when the schedule changes.

What data is used

When a member of staff publishes changes to the schedule, the tool sends a summary email to the staff affected. This email contains only information already held in the school's own cover-planning spreadsheet: staff names, year groups, cover assignments, and the reason for cover (e.g. PPA, training, illness). No pupil data is processed by this notification service.

How email is sent

Notifications are sent using the Gmail API, authorised via Google's OAuth process against a single school Gmail account (wallscourt.scheduling@gmail.com). This tool only requests the gmail.send permission — the narrowest scope Google offers for sending mail. It cannot read, search, or otherwise access the contents of that Gmail account or any other Google account.

Data storage and retention

This service does not maintain its own database of staff or pupil information. The underlying schedule data lives in the school's own Google Sheet, managed directly by school staff. No data submitted through this notification service is shared with, or sold to, any third party, and none of it is used for advertising.
