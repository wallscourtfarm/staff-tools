Your scheduling tools, explained

All the WFA scheduling tools live under one address: staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk. This guide covers what each tool does and how to use it.

Starting point
The Hub
Open tool

The main staff site. The Scheduling & Booking tab (open by default) has all seven scheduling tools. Other tabs cover Planning, Printables, Assessment, Resources, Morning Learning, and Admin.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk
How to use it
1

Go to staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk.

2

The page opens on the Scheduling & Booking tab. Click any tool tile to open it.

3

Use the tabs at the top to browse other sections.

Good to know

Bookmark this address on your iPad or phone so you don't have to type it each day.

Daily operations
Cover & Release Planner
Open tool

Drag-and-drop cover planning. You drag teacher chips to mark them out, and drag cover staff chips onto the gaps. The stats bar shows progress.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/cover-plan/planner.html
How to use it
1

The left panel lists teachers (subtitle: Drag to mark as out). Drag a teacher chip into any session cell in the grid to mark them out. For class teachers, a popup asks you to pick the reason — PPA, training, etc. — with counts showing how many sessions remain.

2

Once a teacher is marked out, their session cells appear with + TBC for any slot that needs cover.

3

The right panel (Cover staff, Drag onto TBC slots) lists available cover staff. The number on each chip shows how many sessions they're already covering this week.

4

Drag a cover staff chip from the right panel and drop it onto a + TBC zone to assign them.

5

The stats bar at the top tracks progress: X sessions out · Y covered · Z TBC · %. The progress bar turns fully green when TBC reaches zero.

6

To unassign cover, click the ✕ on the cover name inside the slot. To delete the whole out-slot, click the ✕ on the slot itself.

7

When ready, click Send Message to notify staff.

Good to know

The toolbar toggle reads 🔒 View only or 🔓 Editing — click it to switch. No PIN required. My view (toolbar) filters the grid to your own sessions only.

Timetabling
Learning Schedules
Open tool

A drag-and-drop timetable builder. Drag subjects from the left panel into the session slots in the grid. Changes save automatically.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/teaching-schedule/
How to use it
1

Use the year group dropdown (top right) to switch between year groups. The tabs below the toolbar switch between the class teacher view (Learning Schedule) and individual Teaching Partner / Learning Partner views.

2

The left panel lists subjects grouped by area — Enquiry, Maths, Reading, Writing Skills, Gatherings. On a TP or LP tab the panel switches to interventions (Reader, Mathematician, Writer, etc.).

3

The grid shows the full week: Morning Registration, Session 1, Fresh Air Break, Session 2, Lunchtime, Session 3, Story Time, and After School. Empty slots say drop here.

4

Drag a subject chip from the left panel and drop it into any session cell. Once placed, the chip has controls: click the name to edit it, ≡ to drag it to another slot, × to remove, ⊞ to split the cell, + to add a note.

5

Use Copy week → next week or Copy to term to replicate the timetable. Clear view (red) removes all subjects from the current tab for the current week.

Good to know

There is no Save button — the schedule saves automatically after every change. A sync indicator in the toolbar shows ● Synced when it's up to date.

Senior Leadership
SLT Schedule
Open tool

A weekly activity planner for SLT. Shows morning and afternoon slots per person, colour-coded by activity type. Viewing is open to all; editing needs a PIN.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/slt-schedule/
How to use it
1

The tool opens in 🔒 View only mode — everyone can see it. Each SLT member has a morning row and an afternoon row for each day. Activity blocks are colour-coded: SLT Time (purple), Teaching (blue), Ops Time (orange), A&B Time (pink), SLT Meeting (indigo), Safeguarding Mtg (red).

2

Blocks marked RS are auto-populated from the Release Schedule — PPA, SENCO Time, Phase Lead Time, Non-working day, Covering Teaching. You can't add or remove these manually; they update automatically.

3

In view mode, clicking an RS block or any block that has a note opens a read-only viewer showing that note. Blocks without notes do nothing when clicked.

4

To edit, click 🔒 View only (top right). A PIN pad appears — enter 2013. The button changes to 🔓 Editing (amber).

5

In edit mode, click the ＋ add button that appears in any empty cell to add an activity. Or click an existing block to edit it. The editor lets you pick an activity type, add a label, and add notes. Click × Remove to delete a block.

6

Use Copy → next week to carry the plan forward. Set as default saves the current week as your standing template; Reset to default restores it.

Good to know

The ⚙ Activities button (toolbar) lets you rename activity types, change their colours, or add new custom ones.

Lunchtime
Lunchtime Leaders
Open tool

A minute-by-minute schedule for lunchtime leaders, showing every person's duties from 11:25 through to checkout. Four views of the same data.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/lunch-leaders/
How to use it
1

The Grid view (default) puts leaders across the columns and times down the left — from 11:25 to around 1:20. Each person's column shows exactly where they should be at every minute of lunch.

2

Switch to Cards for a card-per-leader layout, Leaders or Schedule for other formats. Print produces a printable card sheet.

3

Use the date picker (top toolbar) to view the rota for a specific date.

4

If a leader is absent, click Mark absent under their name. Their column is greyed out and a cover alert appears at the top listing which activities now need attention, flagging critical ones (no one left) in red.

5

If a leader is already marked off-site in the Cover Plan for that day, their column shows 📅 Cover Plan instead — this can't be overridden here.

Good to know

Start shift (the time a leader arrives) is never flagged as needing cover when someone is absent — the tool skips it automatically.

Break times
Fresh Air Break Rota
Open tool

The weekly rota for Fresh Air Break duty. Two year-group bands run at different times. Staff are assigned to specific playground areas by dragging.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/fab-rota/
How to use it
1

The rota has two sections: Y1, Y2, Y3 (10:30–10:45) with a single shared zone, and Y4, Y5, Y6 (10:45–11:00) split across four areas: MUGA, Garden Room (+Bell), Hard Standing, and Trim Trail.

2

The Staff panel (left) lists available staff. Each chip shows their initials and a count of how many sessions they're already assigned this week.

3

To assign someone, drag their chip from the left panel and drop it into a day cell. The + symbols in cells are visual only — you can't click them.

4

To move an assignment, drag the ≡ handle on an existing chip to another cell. To remove, click the × on the chip.

5

For supply staff, dropping onto a cell opens a year-group picker (Y1–Y6 buttons) so you can record which year group they're covering.

6

Once the rota is right, click Set as term rota — new weeks will auto-fill from this template. Use Copy → next week to push the current week forward, or Reset to term rota to revert to the template.

Good to know

If a staff member is marked off-site in the Cover Plan, their chip shows a strikethrough and an ⚠ RS tag. If an entire zone has no unaffected staff, it shows ⚠ Uncovered.

Lunchtime
Lunch Cover Rota
Open tool

Assigns staff to lunchtime zones and activities. Detects scheduling conflicts — including when a duty runs too late for a staff member to make it to their afternoon cover.

staff.wallscourt-farm-academy.co.uk/lunch-cover/
How to use it
1

The rota lists each lunchtime zone with its time: Eating Support (11:50–12:30), KS2 Football, KS2 Special Interest, KS1 Special Interest, EYFS/KS1 Garden Play (two slots), KS2 Packed Lunch Club, KS2 Top Table, Back up in café.

2

Some zones list named pupils — for example Eating Support: Ethan, Haadi, Hugo — so you can assign someone who knows those children.

3

To assign staff, drag a chip from the left panel into a day cell. The + symbols are visual only — not clickable.

4

If a staff member is double-booked across overlapping zones, or if their duty ends too late for them to reach their PM cover class in time, their chip turns red with a 🛑 warning. Hover to see the exact conflict.

5

Use the View as: dropdown (top right) to filter to one staff member — their duties are highlighted and a Your duties this week summary appears below the staff panel.

6

Use Set as term rota to save the template and Copy → next week to roll it forward each week.

Good to know

The ⚙ Duties button (purple, toolbar) lets you add, rename, change times, or delete zones. Changes take effect immediately.
