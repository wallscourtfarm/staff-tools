🫧 Spelling Pop — Pupil Cards

Print, cut out and hand one card to each child. They scan the QR code (or open Spelling Pop and type their code) then enter their PIN.

Proof of concept — QR codes are generated in your browser using the qrcode-generator library (MIT licence, vendored locally in ../vendor/qrcode.js, no external service or network call). PINs live in roster.js alongside this page — treat that file the way you'd treat a class list, not a secrets store.
