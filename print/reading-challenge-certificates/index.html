📚 Reading Challenge Certificates

Wallscourt Farm Academy · prints from the Reading Challenge workbook

1. Choose the workbook

Pick Reading Challenge.xlsx (Staff-Tools › Reading in the shared files). It is read on this device only: nothing is uploaded or saved.

2. Tick the certificates to print

You can add your names file when you print, so the children's names go on the certificates. Without it the certificates show initials.

3. Record them in the workbook

When the certificates have printed, open Reading Challenge.xlsx › Children and type today's date in the matching "certificate given (date)" column for each child below. They then drop off this list.
