How the new scoring works (click to open)

1. For each statement, you pick one status (click a cell). The tool never works the status out for you. These are the same statuses as the new writing tracker.

Non-Negotiables are the previous year's 3 highest-weighted statements, carried up so they stay secure. Each counts at weight 1 and should be secure by Term 2. Year 1's are 4 key EYFS skills, chosen on the Year 1 tab of the review form.

2. Percentages. Expected % = weight earned ÷ weight available across the non-negotiables and expected standards that count. Greater depth % comes from the greater depth statements, which have not been written yet, so no child can show D yet.

3. Grade for a child working at their own year group: D = expected ≥ 90% and greater depth ≥ 60% · O1 = expected ≥ 75% · O2 = expected ≥ 65% · Y = expected ≥ 50%. Below 50% suggests an earlier year's statements: A - Yn. The teacher chooses which year a child is working at.

4. Secure by (new for maths). Each statement has the term it should be secure by: Term 2, 4 or 6. From the term before, a child not yet Secure gets an amber outline (due soon). After that term, they get a red ! (overdue), and the summary row counts them. The term is worked out from today's date; pick another term to see how the grid would look then.

5. Where the numbers come from. Weights and terms come from the review form first, then the tracker's own Weights & targets tab, then a draft value (shown with a dashed box). Wording is the proposed wording unless the review form says "Use: Current". Change them in the review form; this page picks them up when you refresh.
